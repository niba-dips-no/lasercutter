# Workshop files

Built with: `node lasercut.mjs --plates 12 --thickness 3 --dots --housings 2 --keyrings 12 --no-notes --out workshop`

Havsudden peninsula, 1:2000, 12 land plates (+ base, not on the sheet), 3 mm board, forest dots,
2 housings, 12 keyrings, no operator notes. One 1200 × 900 mm sheet.

- `bed-1.svg` / `bed-1.dxf` — for the cutter (red cut, blue score, black etch; 0.025 mm hairlines, roads 0.8 mm)
- `housing.md` — housing parts and assembly (quantities for both housings)
- `plate-N.svg`, `base-guide.svg` — for looking at and gluing
- `index.html` — the interactive page
